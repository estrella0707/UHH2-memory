If you volunteer to do so, you will participate in a survey which investigates decision-making and information processing. This experiment is conducted by researchers from the University of Hamburg.

The experiment is expected to last about 6-10 minutes. You will receive a participation fee of $1.00. Within the survey, you will be provided with an opportunity to earn an additional reward.

During the experiment, you will be asked to provide some basic information about yourself (e.g., age, gender), which will be anonymized. All the data we collect will not allow us to personally identify you.

The data you provide will be anonymized and securely stored, analyzed and reported for scientific purposes only. Summaries may be presented at conferences and included in scientific publications, and the anonymized data will be made available to other researchers. The legal basis for data processing is your consent in accordance with Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a) GDPR.

Participation in this study is entirely voluntary, and you can leave at any time. To receive full payment, you must complete the entire survey. By returning a complete questionnaire, you give your consent to participate. You have the right to withdraw from the study completely. However, once we have anonymized the data by removing all Prolific IDs after the data collection stage, it will become impossible to withdraw your data.

Content warning
This study includes content related to crime, which some individuals may find distressing. You have the option to withdraw from the study now or at any point.
