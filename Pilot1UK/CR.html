Text 2: Air Pollution, Asthma, and Exercise

Both air pollution and exercise increase the risk of asthma. Breathing polluted air causes irritation and asthma, while intense exercise triggers symptoms. So, both air pollution and exercise independently cause asthma symptoms.

Text 3: Eye Strain, Smartphone Use, Distraction

Frequent smartphone-use leads to eye strain from looking at screens for long periods. At the same time, constant notifications and social media make it harder to concentrate, causing more distractions. So, using smartphones independently results in both eye strain and reduced focus.
