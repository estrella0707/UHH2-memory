Research has shown that having stable jobs is linked to a lower risk of committing crimes. Immigrants with little education often face barriers to securing stable jobs. Nonprofit employment programs provide job training, language instruction, and job placement services for immigrants. Hence, they help immigrants develop skills, secure stable jobs. This allows them to support themselves and their families through legal means. Therefore, sufficient support for such a nonprofit employment program reduces crime.
