In this task, you are asked to count the black balls in the specified cell (e.g., Cell D5) in a table.

There are 10 counting questions in total. On each page, you will be presented with one counting question. You have 12 seconds to work on that question. You have to count the number of black balls and enter the correct answer in the box. The page will submit automatically after 12 seconds. Afterward, a new counting question will load.

For each correct answer, you earn a bonus of £0.02. This means that you can earn £0.20 at maximum.

At the top of the page, the remaining time for the task is displayed.

If you are ready, click the “Next” button.
